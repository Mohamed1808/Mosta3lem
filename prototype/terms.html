The service

Mosta3lem is a marketplace. Entities choose a provider for each request or batch; the provider performs the investigation or collection under the entity's instructions. The platform enforces the workflow, tracks deadlines, keeps the audit trail and issues monthly invoices.

Offers and acceptance

A provider has a fixed window, 4 hours by default, to accept or decline an offer. Offers not answered in time expire and the entity may choose another provider. Offers for a batch, or for a governorate group within it, are accepted or declined as a whole.

Service levels

Each request has a due date. The platform warns both sides when 80% of the time has passed and records a breach when the due date passes. Breaches count against the provider's on-time rate and score.

Ratings, scores and enforcement

Entities rate a provider once per closed case or batch. Scores combine operational results with client ratings using weights published by the platform. Providers below published thresholds may be warned, ranked lower, or suspended. Either side can dispute a rating or a case outcome; the platform decides disputes and may remove a rating from a score.

Collections conduct

Collectors act only within the contact methods and settlement authority set by the entity for each case. Settlements outside that authority need the entity's approval before they apply.

Fees and payment

Entities pay the provider's price for each closed case as shown when the offer was sent. The platform keeps a published fee from each provider payout. Invoices are issued monthly per provider.
