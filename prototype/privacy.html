Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review. This page describes the demo build and will be replaced before launch.

Who we are

Mosta3lem connects lenders and other companies (requesting entities) with verified investigation and collection providers in Egypt. The platform routes each request to a provider chosen by the entity and records every step of the work.

What data we process

Entities upload customer details needed for the service: name, national ID, mobile numbers, addresses, employer or business details, contract and amount details. Providers and field agents add field evidence: check-in time and location, photos, report answers, contact attempts, promises to pay and payments.

We also keep account details for users of the platform (name, work email, role) and an audit log of actions taken.

Who can see customer data

Before a provider accepts a request it sees only the service, inquiry type or days-past-due bucket, governorate, amount range, deadline and price. Name, national ID, phone, address and contract number are released only after acceptance, and are masked again 30 days after the case closes.

Other providers never see a case they were not offered. Feedback shown to other entities never says which entity wrote it.

Retention

Case records are kept for the period required by the entity's regulator and our agreements with it. Provider access to personal data ends 30 days after closure.

Your rights

Customers can ask the entity that holds their contract about the data it shared. Platform users can ask us for a copy of their account data or for corrections.
